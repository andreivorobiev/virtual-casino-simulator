# Private Beta Privacy Notice

Requirements: TERMS-004, TERMS-001, TERMS-002 (registered in `docs/requirements/requirements.json`, which is authoritative for their current status).

This privacy notice describes private beta use of the running Virtual Casino Simulator app. The app is a local toy simulator for fake-money play, not a gambling site and not a real-money wagering service.

## Data The App May Store Locally

During local use, the app may store simulated player names, fake balances, play-token ledger entries, game state, game history, bot/autoplay settings, admin telemetry, logs, and test results under the local project data and logs folders.

This data is for simulation, debugging, testing, and admin visibility. It is not payment data, banking data, gambling account data, identity verification data, or real-money wagering history.

## No Payment Or Cash-Out Data

The app does not need or intentionally collect payment card data, bank account data, government identity verification, tax forms, real-money deposit records, withdrawal records, cash-out details, or prize-redemption information.

Play tokens have no cash value and cannot be redeemed, sold, traded, transferred, exchanged, withdrawn, or converted into money, prizes, goods, services, credits, cryptoassets, or anything else of value.

## Local Beta Handling

Private beta data may be reset, deleted, copied into test reports, or inspected during development and validation. Do not enter sensitive personal information, payment information, protected health information, confidential business data, or real gambling activity into the simulator.

## Deployment Boundary

If someone modifies or deploys the project outside the intended local private beta context, they are responsible for updating privacy disclosures, access controls, hosting practices, and legal compliance for that separate use.
